Taking a deposit at TECHM8

A deposit means the customer pays part of the price today. The invoice is saved with the rest still owing, and you collect that balance later through the cart.

All screenshots come from the POS test screen with made-up customers and invoices. Your screen looks the same with real data.

Three rules for every deposit

  • Pick the customer first. Type their name or phone in the customer field at the top right. Without a name and phone you cannot find the invoice again when they come back.
  • Use Confirm Amount, never Full Payment. Full Payment takes the whole total.
  • Finish with the orange button Finish as Deposit. If you close the payment window instead, nothing is saved.

Situation 1

Deposit on a sale or special order

Use this when a customer orders a part or product and pays some of it now. Example: a $499 Galaxy S24 Ultra screen, $100 deposit today.

  1. Select the customer at the top right, add the item to the cart, then tap Checkout.

  2. Choose how they are paying (Cash, Card, Bank Transfer and so on) on the left. Delete the full amount in the box and type the deposit, for example 100.

  3. Take the money (tap the card on the terminal, or count the cash), then tap Confirm Amount.

    The right side now shows Paid $100 and Due $399, and an orange button appears.

  4. Tap Finish as Deposit · $399.00 owing. The POS saves the invoice and shows Deposit Taken.

  5. Tell the customer the balance ($399) and the invoice number. When they come back, follow Customer pays the rest.

Situation 2

Deposit paid with two methods

Example: the customer gives $50 cash and puts $50 on card for a $100 deposit.

  1. In the payment window choose Cash, type 50, tap Confirm Amount.

  2. Choose Card, type 50, take the card payment, tap Confirm Amount.

  3. Both payments are listed on the right. Tap Finish as Deposit.

Store Credit as a depositStore Credit works the same way, but only when a customer with a credit account is selected.
Situation 3

Deposit on a repair card

Use this when the customer pays part of a repair up front, for example for a part that has to be ordered.

  1. On the repair board, tap the cart button on the card (top right of the card) to put the repair in the cart. The card must have the customer's name and phone.

  2. Tap Checkout and take the deposit exactly as in Situation 1: type the amount, Confirm Amount, then Finish as Deposit.

  3. The card now shows an orange Deposit · $X owing tag. Its cart button is hidden until the balance is paid, and the card is not finished yet.

When the repair is readyOpen the card and tap Take balance next to the invoice that is owing (see Customer pays the rest). When the balance is paid in full, the POS takes you through finishing the card.
Situation 4

Deposit by online payment link

Use this when the customer is not in the shop, or wants to pay the deposit from their phone by card or Afterpay. The POS emails them a payment link.

  1. Select the customer (name and phone are required), add the items, tap Checkout.

  2. Choose Online Payment. Type the deposit amount (at least $0.50). The orange line confirms it: Deposit: link for $100.00 · $399.00 stays owing.

  3. Check the customer email, then tap Send link for $100.00. The invoice is saved owing the full amount until the customer pays the link; the link amount comes off when they pay.

Can't send a link?A payment link can't be used when the cart has an earlier invoice balance or an exchange in it. Take those payments in the shop instead.
Situation 5

Customer comes back to pay the rest

Find the invoice that is owing and put its balance in the cart. There are three ways to find it; use whichever is quickest.

From the repair card

Open the card. Under Invoices, tap Take balance.

From the invoice list

Open Sales Performance, search under Find an Invoice, tap the orange cash button on the owing invoice.

From the customer's history

Select the customer, tap the person button beside their name, then tap Pay $X on the owing invoice.

  1. The balance shows in the cart as Balance · Invoice #…, with what was already paid.

  2. Tap Checkout. If they pay it all, use Full Payment or confirm the full amount as normal.

  3. If they can only pay some of it again, type that amount, tap Confirm Amount, then Finish as Part Payment. The rest stays owing on the same invoice.

Situation 6

Paying a balance and buying something new

Put the balance in the cart as in Situation 5, then add the new items as normal. One checkout covers both.

How a part payment is splitNew items are paid first, and whatever is left goes to the old balance. So a part payment must be more than the new items. Here it must be more than $30; if they pay $100, $30 pays the screen protector and $70 comes off the balance.
Situation 7

When the POS won't take a deposit

These are on purpose. Read the message, fix the cart, and try again.
